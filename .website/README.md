## SEVEN CUSHIONS

SEVEN CUSHIONS is a fast arcade ricochet game about turning seven bounces into one explosive rainbow run.

Aim your unicorn, launch, and bank off walls and cushions to smash enemies and build combos. Reach seven bounces to activate RAINBOW mode, then use the remaining momentum to deal massive damage and earn bigger scores.

But where you stop matters. After every shot, enemies take their turn — avoid the red danger zones and land somewhere safe.

Clear all 100 rooms, face a BOSS every 7th room, and build your run with powerful upgrades such as longer Rainbow time, shock waves, extra health, chain kills, bigger hit areas, bonus bounces, and more.

### Controls
- Mouse / A-D — Aim
- Space — Launch / Brake
- Esc — Pause

Clear enemies, chain hits, master the angles, and survive all 100 stages!
